# CollabHub — Pricing & Target Audience

## Target Audience

### Primary: Small-to-Medium Software & Creative Teams (5–50 people)
- Kanban/Scrum boards + sprints cater to development teams
- Document collaboration (TipTap/ProseMirror) suits content and design teams
- CalDAV sync integrates with teams already on Nextcloud or Apple Calendar
- Invoicing module covers agency and studio billing workflows

### Secondary: Freelancers & Agencies
- Invoicing suggests client-facing billing as a core use case
- Project + task management covers end-to-end client project delivery
- Integrated video calls (LiveKit) eliminate the need for Zoom or Google Meet

### Not Suited For
- Enterprise (no SSO/SAML, no audit logs)
- Large organizations (no org-wide admin hierarchy)

## Pricing Tiers

| Tier | Price | Target | Included |
|------|-------|--------|----------|
| **Free** | $0 / mo | Solo / hobbyist | 1 project, 3 users, 1 GB storage |
| **Starter** | $12–15 / mo | Freelancers | 5 projects, 10 users, 10 GB storage |
| **Team** | $29–39 / mo | Small teams | Unlimited projects, 25 users, 50 GB, video calls |
| **Agency** | $69–99 / mo | Agencies | Unlimited everything, invoicing, CalDAV, priority support |

---

## Key Differentiators

- **All-in-one platform** — chat, video, tasks, docs, and invoicing in a single tool (vs. paying separately for Slack + Jira + Notion + Zoom)
- **Self-hostable** — Docker/Sail support appeals to privacy-conscious teams and those with data residency requirements
- **PWA** — mobile-friendly without requiring app store distribution
- **CalDAV** — native calendar sync for teams using Nextcloud, Apple Calendar, or Thunderbird

---

## Sweet Spot

The combination of **invoicing + CalDAV + project management** makes agencies billing clients the highest-value segment. These users need all three layers (delivery, scheduling, billing) and are willing to pay for a unified solution.
